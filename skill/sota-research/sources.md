## Quality Ratings

- **A**: Authoritative, consistently high-quality, primary source
- **A-**: High quality, occasionally less relevant
- **B+**: Good quality, narrower focus or less frequent
- **B**: Useful but verify against A sources
- **C**: Community discussion, treat as anecdotal
